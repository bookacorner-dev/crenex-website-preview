MRI integration questions

Define the scope before rollout.

01

Which data can Crenex exchange with MRI PMX?

The configured mapping can include property and lease references, income categories and booking transaction data. The exact fields, direction and validation rules depend on the customer’s MRI environment and agreed integration scope.

02

Is the integration the same for every MRI customer?

Each deployment is scoped around the customer’s MRI product, version, asset structure and finance process. Supported fields and integration behaviour are confirmed during technical discovery.

03

Which system handles accounting?

MRI PMX remains the property and finance system. Crenex manages the short-term leasing workflow, keeping spaces, bookings, approvals and agreements together and preparing the information required for the finance handoff.

04

What does a rollout involve?

Confirm the MRI environment and business process, agree the field mappings, and test representative bookings and validation rules. Rollout then follows the agreed portfolio scope and operational responsibilities.
