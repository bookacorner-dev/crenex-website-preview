Leasing operations questions

From availability to the finance handoff.

01

Can teams check availability before agreeing a booking?

Crenex shows space occupancy by period, including accepted, active, completed and blocked states. Teams can review the requested dates in that context and start a booking from the availability view.

02

Can approval steps vary by asset or portfolio?

The workflow can reflect the portfolio’s users, permissions, documents and approval requirements. The process is configured for the deployment so each team can work with the rules relevant to its assets.

03

How are agreements and signatures tracked?

Teams can generate agreements using approved templates and retain document and signature events with the booking. The conversation and commercial terms remain available in the same operational record.

04

How does a booking reach finance?

Crenex prepares transaction and income-category data alongside the booking. The finance handoff follows the configured process. For MRI PMX, the agreed fields and validation rules are mapped during integration setup.
