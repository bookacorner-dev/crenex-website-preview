Shopping centre use cases

Give every commercial space a clear process.

Different formats bring different dates, uses and approval requirements. Crenex keeps those details with the space and booking so leasing, operations and finance can work from the same context.

Common-area retail

Kiosks & RMUs

Keep kiosk and retail merchandising unit availability, booking dates and permitted use together. Review requests against the space, then retain the agreed rate, documents and booking owner in one record.

Temporary units

Pop-up shops

Manage a brand’s proposed dates, location and commercial terms alongside the unit’s availability. Follow the request through the centre’s approvals and agreement process, with billing information connected to the booking.

Experiential spaces

Brand activations

Record the proposed activity, space, campaign dates and required documents. Coordinate the local review and keep operational details available to the teams responsible for the booking and on-site activity.

Media inventory

Advertising spaces

Organise advertising inventory by asset, location and format. Keep campaign windows, pricing and booking details together, using the categories and finance process configured for the portfolio.

Portfolio questions

Find the fit for your centres.

01

Who is Crenex designed for?

Crenex supports property owners, operators and leasing teams managing short-term and specialty leasing. Asset, operations and finance teams share the booking context, while leadership can review activity across the portfolio.

02

Can one portfolio include different types of space?

Teams can structure properties, spaces and categories for kiosks, pop-up units, activation areas and advertising inventory. Each booking retains the dates, commercial terms and documents relevant to its use.

03

Can each centre use its own rules?

The deployment can reflect asset-specific space data, users, permissions, documents and approvals. Local teams work with their centre’s requirements while shared reporting provides a portfolio view.

04

How can brands enquire about available spaces?

Crenex Listing provides a branded catalogue for selected spaces and the information you choose to publish. Enquiries enter the leasing workflow for the team to review availability, use and commercial terms.
