Listing + Operations

From a pop-up listing to a leasing enquiry.

An illustrative journey for a brand exploring a temporary space. The catalogue helps it find an opportunity; the enquiry gives the leasing team the context to review the request.

Explore the space

The brand reviews the pop-up location, images and published information in your catalogue, including any date windows and price guidance you choose to show.

Send an enquiry

The brand submits its proposed dates, intended use and the details required by your request form. The enquiry is associated with the relevant asset and workflow.

Review in Operations

The leasing team continues with the same request in Crenex, checking availability and terms before progressing through the configured approvals, agreement and billing process.

Listing questions

Choose how your spaces go to market.

01

Can the catalogue use our branding?

Crenex Listing is configured around the client’s name, visual identity and public-facing experience. Inventory structure, request forms and routing are agreed for the deployment.

02

Do we have to publish prices?

You choose the information shown in the catalogue, including whether to display price guidance and date windows. The leasing team can review the proposed commercial terms with the brand during the enquiry process.

03

Can one catalogue cover several properties?

A client-branded catalogue can present multiple properties, regions and space categories. Enquiries connect to the relevant asset and operational workflow. Explore the shopping centre and portfolio use cases.

04

What happens after a brand sends an enquiry?

The enquiry starts the leasing review. Teams check the requested space, dates and use, then manage the agreed terms, approvals and agreement through Crenex Leasing Operations.
